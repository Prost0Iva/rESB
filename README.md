# Every Single Beast 0.0.1

## Mobs

### Taiga Sapling
The Taiga Sapling is a small live spruce sapling. It can be found in all kinds of taiga, where it wanders and protects the peace of the forest.

### Jellyfish
The Jellyfish can be found in all kinds of ocean. They swim peacefully, drop gelatin when they die, and can be taken into a bucket.

### Iceologer
Do you remember how Mojang wanted to add Iceologer to the game? I decided to bring this idea to an end! Meet Iceologer as I see it. Iceologer spawns in his hut in snowy bioms and drops Ominous Ice Bottle and something else with a slight chance... He also often participates in raids in snowy bioms.

### Bonebeak
Bonebeak is a small skeleton bird that spawns in the snowy plains. It's not very dangerous, but it can become a true threat if you use the **Ominous Ice Bottle** on it!

## Bosses

### Frostybeak
Frostybeak is a creature shrouded in icy terror. He can be summoned by giving the Bonebeak an Ominous Ice Bottle. This is a boss that can be killed in the initial stages of the game.

### Copper Golem
~~The Copper Golem is another mob that failed to win the Mojang vote~~. Now he is no longer so peaceful and a little helper. This is an incredible boss that can be killed in the early stages of the game and get a new kind of material. **Keep in mind**, the worse the weather, the more powerful he is!
